# Paketbote

**Track your parcels from DHL, Hermes, DPD, GLS, UPS and Amazon – on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Linux.**
Inspired by apps like *Parcel*, but your data stays at home: Home Assistant with the integration
[Briefankündigung](https://github.com/Teyro/Briefankuendigung-HA) is the hub. It reads the carriers’ shipping
mails, optionally asks DHL and 17TRACK for live status, and keeps every device in sync.

*Work in progress – see [README.de.md](README.de.md).*
